Questions we hear often

How long does a project take?

Most small websites take one to three weeks and apps take longer. After the first call you get a fixed timeline in writing.

Can you work with Bangla text?

Yes. Websites, apps and PDF reports are built with proper Bangla font rendering, so text never breaks or shows boxes.

Will my app work without internet?

It can. We build apps offline-first, so people keep working and data syncs when the connection returns.

What do I need to start?

Only a clear description of your problem. You do not need designs, a technical plan or a budget figure.

Do you help after launch?

Yes. We fix issues, train your team and add features as your business grows.
